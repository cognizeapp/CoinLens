Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Coinsights ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile application that identifies coins from photographs and estimates their collector value. This policy explains what data the app processes, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

Who is responsible

The data controller is the operator of Coinsights. For any privacy request, contact cognizeapp@gmail.com.

What we process, and why

DataWhyLegal basis
Account details — your email address and display name, when you sign in with Apple, Google or email. To create your account, sign you in, and sync your saved coin collection across your devices. Performance of a contract (providing the service you asked for).
Coin photographs — the pictures you take of a coin. To identify the coin and estimate its value. Photos are processed to produce the identification result and the entries you choose to save to your collection. They are not used for advertising and are never sold. Performance of a contract.
Your collection — the coins you save, plus notes and the identification data attached to them. To show your collection and portfolio value, and sync it to your account. Performance of a contract.
Usage and diagnostic data — app events (e.g. "scan started"), device model, operating-system version, app version, coarse country/region, and crash reports. To understand which features are used, fix bugs and improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you. Legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app.
Subscription status — whether you have an active Coinsights Premium subscription and its renewal date. To unlock Premium features. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or billing details. Performance of a contract.
Advertising data — for users on the free plan only: a device advertising identifier and basic ad-interaction data, used by our advertising provider to show and cap the frequency of ads. To fund the free version. Ads are non-personalised (contextual); we do not build an advertising profile of you and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Premium users see no ads. Legitimate interest in offering a free tier, and your consent where required.

We do not

  • Sell your personal data.
  • Track you across other companies' apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown because we do not track).
  • Access your photo library beyond the pictures you explicitly pick or capture for a scan.
  • Collect your precise location.

Third-party services we use

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and security measures:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) — account, collection sync, usage and diagnostic data. Firebase privacy.
  • Google AI (Gemini API) — when cloud coin identification is enabled, a coin photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the identification. Google states that data submitted through the paid API is not used to train its models. Gemini API terms.
  • RevenueCat — subscription state. RevenueCat privacy.
  • Google AdMob — non-personalised ads for free users. AdMob & privacy.
  • Apple — App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, TestFlight. Apple privacy.

Where data is stored

Data held by Firebase and the services above may be processed on servers in the European Union and the United States. Transfers outside the EU/EEA rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Account and collection data — until you delete your account.
  • Coin photos — the scan image is stored with your saved coin so you can see it later; it is deleted when you delete that coin or your account. Photos sent for cloud identification are processed transiently and not retained by us.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — retained in aggregate for up to 14 months by Firebase Analytics.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account; this permanently removes your account, saved coins and their images. For any other request, or to object to processing, email cognizeapp@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Coinsights is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will update this page and the "last updated" date when this policy changes, and note significant changes in the app.
