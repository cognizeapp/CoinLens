Scan a coin, see what it's worth

Point your camera at a coin. Coinsights identifies it and gives an estimated collector value — with the history, rarity and a plan for selling it.

What it does

  • Identify a coin from a photo: country, year, denomination, material.
  • Estimated market-value range, explained factor by factor.
  • Confirm the details yourself for an accurate, catalogue-based value.
  • Save coins to a digital collection and see your portfolio value.
  • Rankings: the most valuable coins in the world, the rarest, and key dates to check your change for.
  • Premium adds a full AI history, rarity & condition analysis, a where-to-sell guide, and an ad-free experience.
