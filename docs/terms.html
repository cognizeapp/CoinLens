What Coinsights is

Coinsights helps you identify coins from photographs and gives an estimated collector value, together with historical and selling information. It is an informational tool for hobbyists.

No professional advice — important

  • Coinsights is not a professional coin grading, authentication or appraisal service, and its output is not a certification of any kind.
  • Values shown are estimates generated from catalogue data and automated analysis. Real market value depends on condition, authenticity, mint variety, provenance and current demand, and can differ substantially from the estimate.
  • Identification can be wrong. Always confirm the details yourself and, for anything potentially valuable, consult a qualified numismatist or grading service before buying, selling or insuring a coin.
  • Nothing in the app is financial or investment advice.
  • Never clean a collectible coin — it usually destroys value. Any tips in the app are general information, not instructions for your specific coin.
