# Coinsights — App Store Optimization (ASO)

Complete store listing copy for the App Store and Google Play. Character
budgets are noted `[n]`; stay at or under. Non-EN/IT sets should get a
native-speaker pass before submission.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-liner:** Point your camera at a coin and find out what it's really worth.

**Who it's for:** people who find old coins at home and wonder if they're
valuable; hobby and serious coin collectors; anyone thinking of selling
inherited coins.

**The hook:** free identification + value estimate answers "how much could this
be worth?" in seconds. Premium adds the story, rarity, condition and a selling
plan.

**Primary keyword targets:** coin identifier, coin value, coin scanner,
what's my coin worth, coin appraisal, numismatics, rare coins.

**Comparable apps (for keyword mining / differentiation):** Coin ID Scanner,
CoinSnap, Coinoscope, PCGS Photograde, Numista. Coinsights' angle: *value-first
and transparent* — it explains the estimate factor by factor instead of just
showing a number, and has a "most valuable coins" discovery feed.
